## What Infuser does

We put AI inside the operations of B2B companies. We map how the work really runs, set up a second brain over the company's approved sources, and automate the processes that slow the team down.

I lead the technical side: the systems, the integrations and the agents.

<br/>

## Catalyst Protocol

**Why it exists.** Automating a process nobody has mapped only makes its waste run faster. What people say they do and what they actually do can differ, and the gap is where the cost hides. So before we build anything, we look.

<p align="center"><img src="assets/catalyst-protocol-flow.png" alt="Catalyst Protocol flow" width="100%"></p>

**What you get:** a map of how the process runs today, a proposed flow, a ranked list of opportunities and a plan to compare before and after.

<br/>

## What we build

- **Ops panel + conversational second brain** over the company's approved sources
- **Process hub** where the team runs its routines, with owners, human decisions and a way out for exceptions
- **Integrations and agents** for ERPs, CRMs and systems without an API
- **Scheduled routines** that read, reconcile and post results where the team already looks

Client systems are private. What is public is the products we also sell at [useinfuser.com](https://useinfuser.com):
